    Assumptions and scope
    • Variables have a type and a distribution; labels such as treatment or outcome impose nothing. Any directed acyclic graph is allowed.
    • Continuous: Normal, Uniform, Log-normal, Gamma. Binary: Bernoulli. Count: Poisson. Survival time: Weibull or Exponential with proportional hazards.
    • An arrow adds the parent, times its coefficient, to the child’s predictor on the scale shown in its equation; interactions add products of two parents.
    • Survival variables are latent event times. A cutoff or another variable as the censoring time adds observed-time and event columns; shared parents make censoring dependent.
    • Independent-observation designs generate independent rows. Structured designs retain subject and cluster variables across visits, with explicit shared effects, residual correlation and lagged relationships.
    • Power is conditional on the chosen generating distributions, response functions, dependence and observation process. Monte Carlo intervals describe simulation error, not uncertainty about these assumptions.
    • Power simulates whole studies from the graph and analyzes each with the method the panel shows. An effect to detect recalibrates one arrow for the run; the drawn graph is kept.
    • Models stay in this browser and can be exported as JSON; datasets download as CSV.
